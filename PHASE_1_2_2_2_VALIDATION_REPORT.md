- **Exact Citation:** **CS(COMM) 1028/2024**, High Court of Delhi, Interim Order dated 24 July 2026.
- **Substantive Judicial Findings Accurately Represented:**
  1. *Prima Facie Finding on Training Storage:* The Delhi High Court made a **prima facie finding that OpenAI's temporary storage of ANI's works for LLM training fell within the scope of Section 52(1)(a)** of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957.
  2. *Denial of Injunction:* The Court **declined to grant interim injunctive relief** against OpenAI's training storage.
  3. *Non-Infringing RAG Outputs:* The Court found, on the record and evidence before it, that **ChatGPT's synthesized RAG outputs did not amount to copyright infringement**.
- **Crucial Procedural Qualification:**
  - These determinations were made strictly at the **interim interlocutory stage** under Order XXXIX Rules 1 & 2 CPC.
  - **They do not constitute a final decree or binding decision on the merits of the suit.**
  - Full trial remains pending regarding large-scale commercial AI training, database extraction, and model memorization.
